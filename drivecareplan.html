DRIVECAREPLAN™

STAY INFORMED. STAY AHEAD.

DriveCarePlan is a system that combines car monitoring, analytics, and preventative service. It automatically tracks the status of your vehicle (mileage, fuel consumption, ECU errors, temperatures, load, etc.) and reminds you about necessary maintenance, diagnostics, or refueling.

The plan integrates with our telematics devices and helps us remotely analyze the technical condition of your car 24/7.

SPECIFICATIONS

01

24/7 TELEMATICS MONITORING

Via a telematics module, your car is constantly monitored for errors. Data is sent directly from your car's ECU, and any faults that appear are immediately reflected in our system.

02

AUTOMATIC ERROR NOTIFICATIONS

Our technicians analyze the severity of the problem and notify you if diagnostics or repair are needed.

03

DRIVING AND EFFICIENCY ANALYTICS

Analysis of fuel consumption, engine load, and driving style. If you suddenly see increased fuel consumption, it might indicate a need to check your tires, perform a wheel alignment, replace filters, etc., as these factors affect your engine, suspension, and costs.

04

MILEAGE AND SCHEDULED MAINTENANCE REMINDERS

We understand life is fast and you might forget about car maintenance (like when it's time to change oil or filters). Our system receives notifications when your scheduled maintenance is due. We determine this interval with you—every 6,000, 8,000, or 10,000 km, depending on the car's mileage, make, type, etc. Our technicians will suggest the correct interval. We will notify you automatically when it's time for maintenance.

05

LOW FUEL CONCIERGE

If your fuel level drops below 5 L, you receive an SMS: "Refuel now?" A courier will come for free and refuel your car directly at your home or work. This service operates within Calgary and its surroundings. You only pay for the cost of the gasoline (Shell, Costco, Esso, Chevron, Petro Canada).

06

PRIORITY SERVICE

You receive priority scheduling for service.

07

FREE PICKUP & RETURN

Free PickUp & Return of your car for diagnostics.

08

MONTHLY TECHNICAL REPORT

You receive a monthly technical report on your vehicle.
