How It Works

Step 1

Investment Plan & Start

The investor meets with us and receives a detailed action plan — how exactly we buy, repair and sell cars, and what return to expect.

After reviewing the plan, the investor contributes funds with a full understanding of the process.

Goal: full clarity and confidence before investing.
Step 2

Car Purchase at Auction

We buy cars only from trusted, official auctions — minimizing risk at every stage. Each vehicle is checked before bidding.

Our experienced team knows how to choose the right cars and avoid mistakes. If any issue occurs, we handle it.

Goal: safe purchase with zero unnecessary risks.
Step 3

Repair & Preparation for Sale

We work with our own service network and verified partners to restore each vehicle, keeping quality high and costs controlled.

Thanks to our methods and local expertise, we can sell affordably for buyers while keeping strong margins.

Goal: maximize value through quality and efficiency.
Step 4

Selling at Market Price

After full restoration, the car is listed for sale through our trusted sales channels.

Our marketing and reputation help us close sales within days. Each sale generates transparent financial results.

Goal: fast sale at fair market value.
