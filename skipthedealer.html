Customers can get a car 30–40% cheaper than the market price. There are two main scenarios:

1.Buy and profit

A customer buys a car for $10,000, while the market price is $14,000–$15,000. They immediately gain $4,000–$5,000 in value.

2. Drive without losing

A customer keeps the car for 2–3 years. During this time, the market price might drop from $15,000 to $7,000–$8,000 — but since they bought it for $10,000, the loss is zero.

With SkipTheDealer, customers either profit or minimize losses to zero.

How It Works

Step 1. Introduction & Car Selection

You tell us about your dream car — make, model, year, body type, features, and budget.

At this stage, you immediately receive an approximate financial overview:

Approximate purchase price at auctionEstimated cost to buy the car at auction.
Repair costsExpected expenses to fix any damage.
Additional expensesInspection, diagnostics, registration, fees.
Final “turnkey” priceTotal cost to have the car ready to drive.

We always calculate the total with your expected savings in mind—your car ends up 30–40% cheaper than dealer or marketplace prices.

Example: if the market price of a car is $20,000, we typically buy it at auction for around $10,000. Then we add repair, inspection, fees, and registration costs — and the final price comes to about $14,000. As a result, you save $6,000 compared to the market.

Thanks to this transparency, even before buying you know: - how much the car itself will cost, - what the repair and documentation expenses will be, - what the total final price is, - and how much money you’ll actually save.

Step 2. Daily Selection & Auctions

Every day, we select several cars that match your requirements:

Daily selectionWe pick cars that match your requirements.
Detailed analysisYou get photos, history, and estimated costs for each car.
Your confirmationYou choose which cars meet your needs.
Auction biddingWe place bids on the selected cars.

Auctions are competitive—sometimes a car is not won immediately. We keep searching until we secure a vehicle within your budget, usually within a week.

Step 3. Purchase & Restoration

After winning the auction, your car is delivered to our service center for a full inspection:

If the car is intact → we perform a complete check and fix all technical issues.

If damaged (collision, hail, cosmetic) → we do bodywork, painting, and full repairs.

The result: a car in perfect condition.

Step 4. Inspection & Documents

If the car is clean (active status) → you can register it immediately.

If the car has salvage status → it undergoes an AMVIC inspection and gets rebuilt status.

In all cases, you receive official documents confirming the car’s safety and functionality.
