Dealers are out of the game. Now it’s your turn.

Customers can get a car 30–40% cheaper than the market price. There are two main scenarios:

1. Buy and profit

A customer buys a car for $10,000, while the market price is $14,000–$15,000. They immediately gain $4,000–$5,000 in built-in savings. If they choose to resell the car right away, they can essentially earn this money.

2. Drive without losing

A customer keeps the car for 2–3 years. During this time, the market price might drop from $15,000 to $10,000. But since they bought the car for $10,000, they can sell it for the same amount without losing anything.

With SkipTheDealer, customers either profit or minimize losses to zero.

HOW IT WORKS

Step 1. Introduction & Car Selection

You tell us about your dream car — make, model, year, body type, features, and budget. At this stage, you immediately receive an approximate financial overview:

Approximate purchase price at auction — Estimated cost to buy the car at auction.

Repair costs (if the car has any damage) — Expected expenses to fix any damages.

Additional expenses — Extra costs for inspections, diagnostics, registration, and fees.

Final “turnkey” price — Total cost to have the car completely ready to drive.

We always calculate the total with your expected savings in mind — your car ends up 30–40% cheaper than dealer or Marketplace prices.

Example: if the market price of a car is $20,000, we typically buy it at auction for around $10,000. Then we add repair, inspection, fees, and registration costs — and the final price comes to about $14,000. As a result, you save $6,000 compared to the market.

  • ✓ What the repair and documentation expenses will be
  • ✓ What the total final price is
  • ✓ And how much money you’ll actually save

Step 2. Daily Selection & Auctions

Every day, we select several cars that match your requirements:

Daily selection — We handpick matching vehicles across licensed wholesale auctions.

Detailed analysis — You get high-res photos, CarFax history, and estimated costs for each car.

Your confirmation — You choose which cars meet your exact expectations.

Auction bidding — We place expert bids on the confirmed cars within your budget.

Auctions are competitive — sometimes a car is not won immediately. We keep searching and bidding until we secure the right one within your target budget.

Step 3. Purchase & Restoration

After winning the auction, your car is delivered to our service center for a full restoration:

If the car is intact →

We perform a complete mechanical check, full fluid maintenance, and fix all technical issues.

If damaged (collision, hail, cosmetic) →

We do certified bodywork, laser geometry alignment, OEM painting, and full restoration in our dedicated shop.

The result: a car in perfect condition.

Step 4. Inspection & Documents

We take care of all paperwork, regulatory approvals, and insurance certification:

If the car is clean (active status) →

You can register it immediately with clean Canadian title.

If the car has salvage status →

It undergoes a rigorous AMVIC inspection and receives official certified rebuilt status.

In all cases, you receive official documents confirming the car’s safety and full compliance with Canadian standards.
