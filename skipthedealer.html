Customers can get a car 30–40% cheaper than the market price. There are two main scenarios:

1. Buy and profit

A customer buys a car for $10,000, while the market price is $14,000–$15,000. They immediately gain $4,000–$5,000 in equity and save 30% or more from day one.

HOW IT WORKS

Step 1. Introduction & Car Selection

You tell us about your dream car — make, model, year, body type, features, and budget.

At this stage, you immediately receive an approximate financial overview:

Auction Price

Estimated cost to buy the car directly at auction.

Repair Costs

Expected expenses to fix any cosmetic or technical damage.

Additional Fees

Extra costs for inspections, diagnostics, registration, and documentation.

Final Turnkey

Total turnkey cost to have the car registered and ready to drive.

We always calculate the total with your expected savings in mind:

Example: If the market price of a car is $20,000, we typically buy it at auction for around $10,000. Adding repair, inspection, fees, and registration costs, the final price comes to about $14,000. You save $6,000 compared to market.

Step 2. Daily Selection & Auctions

Every day, our specialists hunt and select several cars that strictly match your requirements:

Daily Selection

We pick verified vehicles matching your exact criteria.

Detailed Analysis

You get complete photos, history reports, and itemized cost estimates.

Your Confirmation

You choose and confirm which specific cars we place bids on.

Auction Bidding

We represent you at competitive closed dealer auctions.

Auctions are competitive — sometimes a car is not won immediately. We keep searching until we secure a vehicle within your budget, usually within a week.

Step 3. Purchase & Restoration

After winning the auction, your car is delivered to our certified Calgary service center for a full inspection:

If the car is intact

We perform a complete mechanical check, full diagnostics, and fix all minor technical issues.

If damaged (collision, hail, cosmetic)

We do precision robotic bodywork, PaintBot painting, and comprehensive mechanical repairs.

The result: A car in perfect condition.

Step 4. Inspection & Documents

Official documentation and certified provincial registration ready for driving:

Clean Active Status

If the car has clean active status, you can register and insure it immediately.

Salvage Status to Rebuilt

Undergoes rigorous AMVIC inspection and receives certified rebuilt active roadworthy status.

In all cases, you receive complete official documents confirming the vehicle’s safety, mechanical integrity, and legal registration.
